RESEARCH QUESTION · HOOK & GAP

Less pollution, but still unaffordable — which path?

APublic debate

Is clean heating
good?

Regional stats dominate; household income, housing, and heating costs differ.

BThis study

Which coal-to-X route
fits this household?

Interactive comparison of gas / heat-pump routes, with compliance and subsidy shocks.

Policy success can coexist with household affordability pressure.

Scope

Converted and unconverted clean-heating farmers in North China; students and other interested public users.

Hypotheses

H1 Farmers can find a coal-to-X path that fits their own household. H2 Students and other users improve their understanding after using the sandbox.

03 · BACKGROUND
03 / 09
CONTEXT · WHY THIS MATTERS

Clean heating is not only
an environmental question.

Related Research Fields

Rural energy transition · clean heating policy · energy affordability · environmental governance

Situation

Since 2016, many rural households in North China shifted from coal to cleaner heating.

Importance

Cleaner air can come with higher winter bills; village-level choices must fit diverse household budgets.

Why Ordinary Readers Should Care

Heating is a basic need. The sandbox makes the trade-off between clean air, cost, and practical village choices easier to see.

04 · LITERATURE REVIEW
04 / 09
RESEARCH PROGRESS · GAP · CONTRIBUTION

Evidence is strong.
Decision support is still thin.

Research Progress

Previous studies show clear pollution and carbon benefits, while policy simulation can connect household behavior with larger outcomes.

Liu et al. · Yuan et al. · Wang

Limitations

Affordability, infrastructure, fiscal pressure, price risk and coal rebound are recognized, but most work remains regional or policy-level.

Few tools compare gas, electricity, heat pumps and insulation from household inputs.

My Contribution

I turn the policy problem into an interactive village-level sandbox using income, house size, heating cost and affordability pressure.

The question shifts from “Is clean heating good?” to “Which coal-to-X path fits this village?”
